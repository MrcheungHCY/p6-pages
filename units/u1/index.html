Sentence structures · 句式

Colour key: subject · structure word · verb · object / complement

1 · told + someone + to + V

someone told someone to V
例子 · doctor
The doctor told me to take the medicine.
例子 · parent
Mum told Tom to drink more water.
例子 · teacher
The teacher told us to rest at home.

2 · told + someone + not to + V

someone told someone not to V
例子 · doctor
The doctor told Amy not to eat ice cream.
例子 · parent
Dad told me not to play football today.
例子 · nurse
The nurse told him not to touch the wound.

3 · should / ought to + V (illness advice)

you should / ought to V
例子 · should
You should see a doctor if you have a fever.
例子 · ought to
You ought to take a rest when you have a headache.
例子 · should
Ken should drink warm water for his cough.

Notes · 筆記

  • Talk about common illnesses (flu, cough, fever, headache…).
  • Doctor / parent: told me to… / told me not to… — see Sentence structures.
  • Advice: should / ought to + V when someone feels unwell.
  • Useful classroom phrase: What's the matter with you?
