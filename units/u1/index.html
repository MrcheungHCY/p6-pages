Sentence structures · 句式

Colour key: subject · structure word · verb · object / complement

1 · told + someone + to + base verb

Use this to retell an instruction. to is followed by the base verb (take, drink, rest) — not -ing, not past.

someone told someone to base verb
例子 · doctor
The doctor told me to take the medicine.
例子 · parent
Mum told Tom to drink more water.
例子 · teacher
The teacher told us to rest at home.
例子 · his
The dentist told Tom to brush his teeth.

2 · told + someone + not to + base verb

Put not before to. The next word is still the base verb. 唔好寫 not to eating，亦唔好寫 to not。

someone told someone not to base verb
例子 · doctor
The doctor told Amy not to eat ice cream.
例子 · parent
Dad told me not to play football today.
例子 · nurse
The nurse told him not to touch the wound.
例子 · my
Mum told me not to rub my eyes.

3 · Hidden trap · your → my / his / her / their

When you retell the sentence with told … to, your changes to match the person who was told. my (the speaker’s thing) becomes his or her. 轉述唔好留 your。呢個係常見陷阱。

Direct · me
Mum told me, “Wash your hands.”
Reported · your → my
Mum told me to wash my hands.
Direct · Tom
The teacher told Tom, “Bring your book.”
Reported · your → his
The teacher told Tom to bring his book.
Direct · Amy
The nurse told Amy, “Take your medicine.”
Reported · your → her
The nurse told Amy to take her medicine.
Direct · the children
Dad told the children, “Pack your bags.”
Reported · your → their
Dad told the children to pack their bags.
Direct · speaker’s my
Mum told Tom, “Don’t use my cup.”
Reported · my → her · Don’t → not to
Mum told Tom not to use her cup.
our · look at the owner
Mr and Mrs Lee told Amy, “Please feed our cat.”
Reported · our → their
Mr and Mrs Lee told Amy to feed their cat.
The cat is theirs, not Amy’s. If Mum says “Tidy our room” to us, our stays our.

4 · should / ought to + base verb (illness advice)

should + base verb. Do not add to. ought to already includes to — do not add a second one. Both mean advice. 唔好寫 should to，亦唔好寫 ought see，亦唔好寫 ought to to。

you should base verb · you ought to base verb
例子 · should
You should see a doctor if you have a fever.
例子 · ought to
You ought to take a rest when you have a headache.
例子 · should
Ken should drink warm water for his cough.
Trap · no to after should
✗ You should to see a doctor.
✓ You should see a doctor.
Trap · ought needs to
✗ You ought take a rest.
✓ You ought to take a rest.

Notes · 筆記

This unit is about feeling unwell: name the illness, retell what the doctor or a parent told someone to do, and give advice.

呢個 unit 學生病嘅字、點樣轉述醫生或者爸媽嘅吩咐，同埋用 should / ought to 畀意見。

Classroom line: What’s the matter with you? / What’s wrong?

Illness words

  • flu 流感 · cough 咳 · fever 發燒 · headache 頭痛
  • stomachache 肚痛 · toothache 牙痛 · backache 背痛 · sore throat 喉嚨痛
  • runny nose 流鼻水 · dizzy 頭暈 · medicine 藥 · rest 休息

told … to

Use this to retell an instruction. to + base verb.

The doctor told me to take the medicine
Doctor
The doctor told me to take the medicine.
Parent
Mum told Tom to drink more water.
Dentist · his
The dentist told Tom to brush his teeth.

told … not to

not to + base verb. Don’t write not to eating, not run, or to not run.

Doctor
The doctor told Amy not to eat ice cream.
Dad
Dad told me not to play football today.
Fix
✗ The doctor told Ken not to running.
✓ The doctor told Ken not to run.

should / ought to

Both give advice. should + base verb, with no extra to. ought to already includes to — do not add another. He / she does not add -s to should or ought.

should
You should see a doctor if you have a fever.
ought to
You ought to stay in bed when you have a headache.
should not
You should not drink cold drinks when you have a sore throat.

“If you have a fever” here only means “when you are ill”. Unit 3 teaches If…will properly.

Hidden traps & tricks

隱藏陷阱／小技巧 · possessive pronouns

轉述做 told … to 嗰陣，引號入面嘅代名詞要跟主人轉。最常見係留低 your。唔好咁寫。

  • your = the listener. told me → my · told Tom → his · told Amy → her · told us → our · told the children → their.
  • my = the speaker. Mum’s my → her. Dad’s my → his.
  • our: if it is only the speakers’ thing, change it to their. If the listener is in the group, keep our.
  • Don’t → not to + base verb. Drop the quotation marks in the reported sentence.
your → my
✗ Mum told me to wash your hands.
✓ Mum told me to wash my hands.
your → his
✗ The teacher told Tom to bring your book.
✓ The teacher told Tom to bring his book.
your → her
✗ The nurse told Amy to take your medicine.
✓ The nurse told Amy to take her medicine.
your → their
✗ Dad told the children to pack your bags.
✓ Dad told the children to pack their bags.
my → her
Direct: Mum told Tom, “Don’t use my cup.”
✓ Mum told Tom not to use her cup.
our → their / our stays
Mr and Mrs Lee told Amy to feed their cat.
Mum told us to tidy our room. (we share it)
not to + base verb
✗ told Ken not to to run / not running
✓ told Ken not to run
should / ought to
✗ should to see · ought see · ought to to see
✓ should see · ought to see
