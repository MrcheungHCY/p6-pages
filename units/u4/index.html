Sentence structures · 句式

Colour key: subject · structure word · verb · object / idea

1 · unless + present clause (= if not)

unless means if not. The unless-clause uses the present, not will. Do not add another not — unless already contains “not”, so a second one flips the meaning. If Unless starts the sentence, put a comma after the condition. unless 即係 if not。唔好寫 unless … not，亦唔好寫 unless … will。句首要加逗號。

situation unless condition (present)
Unless condition, situation
例子 · playground
The playground will stay messy unless we pick up the lunch boxes.
same meaning · if not
The playground will stay messy if we do not pick up the lunch boxes.
例子 · stream
Fish cannot live in this stream unless the water is clean.
例子 · fans
The fans will keep running unless someone switches them off.
Unless first · comma
Unless we bring our own bottles, we will buy more plastic ones.
例子 · garden
Unless it rains this week, the school garden will stay dry.

2 · … so that + clause (purpose)

so that answers “why / for what purpose”. After it, use a subject + can or will. It is not because (a reason that is already true) and it is not so alone (a result). so that 講目的。because 講原因。單獨 so 好多時係結果。

action so that someone can / will purpose
例子 · purpose
We close the tap tightly so that we can save water.
例子 · purpose
Bring a cloth bag so that you will not need a plastic one.
例子 · purpose
The class sorts the bottles so that the cleaners can recycle them.
例子 · purpose
I switch off the monitor so that I can save energy.
because = reason
The pond is cloudy because people left snack packets in the water.
so = result
It rained heavily, so the picnic was cancelled.

Notes · 筆記

This unit is about looking after the Earth: name environmental problems, talk about eco-friendly actions, and write a brochure.

呢個 unit 教污染同環境問題、保護地球嘅行動，同埋宣傳冊（brochure）嘅寫法。

Nine core words

Say them clearly, especially words ending in -tion (pollution, destruction, extinction). 九個核心詞要識讀、識寫、識用。

  • climate change 氣候變化 — The world’s usual weather becomes hotter and more extreme over many years.
  • habitat destruction 棲息地破壞 — The natural places where animals live are damaged or taken away.
  • animal extinction 動物絕種 — A kind of animal dies out and disappears forever.
  • land pollution 土地污染 — Rubbish or harmful chemicals make the ground dirty and unsafe.
  • air pollution 空氣污染 — Dirty smoke or gases make the air harmful to breathe.
  • water pollution 水質污染 — Rivers, lakes or the sea become dirty and unsafe.
  • save energy 節約能源 — Use less electricity and fuel, and do not waste power.
  • plant trees 種樹 — Grow more trees to help the Earth and give animals homes.
  • reduce waste 減少廢物 — Make less rubbish by using fewer things, reusing and recycling.
climate change
Scientists say climate change is making our summers longer and hotter.
habitat destruction
Habitat destruction happens when people cut down a hillside forest.
animal extinction
Some rare frogs are in danger of animal extinction if their streams dry up.
land / air / water
Snack packets on the field cause land pollution. Smoke from idling cars is air pollution. Oil and plastic in the typhoon shelter is water pollution.
save / plant / reduce
Please save energy by switching off the hall lights. We plant trees on the slope so birds can nest again. A reusable bottle helps us reduce waste.

Other useful phrases

  • damage 破壞／損害 — Do not damage the flowers along the path.
  • wild animals’ habitat 野生動物嘅棲息地 — A new bike trail should not cut through wild animals’ habitat.
  • major factor 主要因素 — Old school buses can be a major factor in air pollution near the gate.
  • make an effort 努力／盡力 — We should make an effort to switch off the fans after assembly.
  • eco-tours 生態旅遊 — Write the hyphen. Say EE-koh tours. Our class may join eco-tours at a country park.

unless = if not

The playground will stay messy unless we pick up the lunch boxes

Same meaning: The playground will stay messy if we do not pick up the lunch boxes.

water
Fish cannot live in this stream unless the water is clean.
fans
The fans will keep running unless someone switches them off.
walk
Tom will miss the hillside walk unless he leaves home early.

so that = purpose

purpose
We close the tap tightly so that we can save water.
purpose
We walk to the park so that we can cut car smoke.
because · reason
The pond is cloudy because people left snack packets in the water.
so · result
It rained heavily, so the picnic was cancelled.

so that 要有主語，後面多數 can / will。so that 唔係 because，亦唔係單獨一個 so。

Brochure language

A brochure promotes a product, a service, or an event. Use these features when you write one.

verb to start
Join the hillside clean-up this Saturday.
Bring your own water bottle.
you
You can plant a seedling at the end of the walk.
You will see dragonflies near the pond.
question
Have you ever walked under tall trees near Tsuen Wan?
Would you like to help a local park this Saturday?
so that
Come early so that you can find a seat on the minibus.
Wear covering shoes so that you will stay safe on the path.
Hidden traps & tricks

隱藏陷阱／小技巧

默書同寫作都要小心呢五個陷阱。unless 後面通常用肯定形式。so that、because 同 so 唔好撈亂。

  • unless = if not. The words after unless are usually positive. Do not write unless we do not…
  • Present tense after unless. Do not write unless … will.
  • so that = purpose. because = reason. so alone often = result.
  • habitat is the place. habitat destruction is the action of damaging that place.
  • eco-tours needs a hyphen. Say EE-koh tours. wild animals’ has the apostrophe after s.
unless = if not
✓ messy unless we pick up the lunch boxes
✓ messy if we do not pick them up
no second not
✗ The stream will stay dirty unless we do not clean it.
✓ The stream will stay dirty unless we clean it.
no will after unless
✗ unless someone will turn them off
✓ unless someone turns them off
so that ≠ because
✗ The pond is cloudy so that people left snack packets there.
✓ The pond is cloudy because people left snack packets there.
habitat ≠ destruction
✗ The egrets lost their habitat destruction.
✓ The egrets lost their habitat.
✓ Cutting the wetland is habitat destruction.
eco-tours · animals’
✗ ecotours · wild animals habitat
✓ eco-tours · wild animals’ habitat
