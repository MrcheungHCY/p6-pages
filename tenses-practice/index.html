  3. Write / choose the FULL answer: future = will + verb; present perfect = has/have + past participle; continuous = is/am/are + -ing; negation = three words (e.g. did not drink).

Teaching reference — 5 tenses & time words (tap to open)
Tense Form Time words
1. Present simple he/she/it → +s · I/you/we/they → base always, usually, often, sometimes, seldom, rarely, never, every day / morning / week / year, on Mondays…, once a week, twice a week
2. Present continuous is / am / are + -ing now, right now, at the moment, at present, Look!, Listen!, these days
3. Past simple regular -ed · irregular (went, saw, ate…) yesterday, last night/week/month/year, … ago, just now, the day before yesterday, in 2020…
4. Future will + verb (write both words; do not leave out will) tomorrow, next week/month/year, soon, later, tonight, the day after tomorrow, in the future
5. Present perfect has / have + past participle (write both words) already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for, recently, lately, so far, up to now
Important: just now = past · standalone just = present perfect. never can be present habit or present perfect — read the whole sentence. When the tense changes from the previous blank, look for a time word in that sentence.
