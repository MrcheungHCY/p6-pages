# P.6 Unit 2 Grammar sheet · Good Habits

**Target:** **ought to** / **should** · **-ing** as subject · **need to be** + adj

## 1 · ought to / should + V

- We **ought to** do housework every day.
- Students **should** be punctual for school.
- You **should** exercise regularly to stay healthy.

## 2 · -ing as subject + verb + …

- **Doing housework** helps the family.
- **Getting up early** makes us more energetic.
- **Sharing snacks** shows that we are generous.

## 3 · need to be + adjective

- Class monitors **need to be** responsible.
- We **need to be** patient when we wait in a queue.
- Clumsy friends **need to be** more careful with cups.
