# P.6 Unit 1 Grammar sheet · Feeling Unwell

**Target:** **told** to / **told** **not to** · **should** / **ought to** (illness advice)

## 1 · told + someone + to + V

- The doctor **told** me **to** take the medicine.
- Mum **told** Tom **to** drink more water.
- The teacher **told** us **to** rest at home.

## 2 · told + someone + not to + V

- The doctor **told** Amy **not to** eat ice cream.
- Dad **told** me **not to** play football today.
- The nurse **told** him **not to** touch the wound.

## 3 · should / ought to + V

- You **should** see a doctor if you have a fever.
- You **ought to** take a rest when you have a headache.
- Ken **should** drink warm water for his cough.
