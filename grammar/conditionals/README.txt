QUOTES USED (wording checked online)
1. 《大話西遊之仙履奇緣》(1995), 至尊寶（周星馳）:
   「曾經有一份真誠的愛情放在我面前，我沒有珍惜，等我失去的時候我才後悔莫及，人世間最痛苦的事莫過於此。」
   + bonus line「如果上天能夠給我一個再來一次的機會，我會……」
   Sources: https://www.zhouxingchifans.com/t53.html (script)
            https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E7%88%B1%E4%BD%A0%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%87%E5%B9%B4/2726094
   English on the page: "If I had treasured it, I would not have lost it."
                        "If I had said "I love you", I would not have regretted it."
                        Bonus (2nd cond.): "If Heaven gave me one more chance, I would say three words to her."
   The sentence about the sword at his throat was left out on purpose.
2. 《少林足球》(2001), 阿星（周星馳）: 「做人如果冇夢想，同條鹹魚有咩分別呀？」
   Sources: https://enjoymovie.net/zh-hk/movies/M7pxz2nA-d/shaolin-soccer/quotes/23ONydLAMR
            https://ballson.blog.fc2.com/blog-entry-10.html (Cantonese script: 同啲鹹魚…)
            https://qt.100ke.info/wiki/%E5%B0%91%E6%9E%97%E8%B6%B3%E7%90%83 (…有咩分別呢?)
   (Sources differ slightly: 條/啲, 呀/呢.) English: "If you have no dreams, you are just a salted fish!"
3. 《食神》(1996), 史提芬周（周星馳）: 「只要有心，人人都是食神！」
   Source: https://zh.wikiquote.org/wiki/%E9%A3%9F%E7%A5%9E
   English (Meme Lab): "If you cook with your heart, you will become a God of Cookery!"
