Health

8 words

backache

n.

Meaning: a pain in your back

After I carried the heavy school bag all day, I had a backache.

cough

n.

Meaning: a sudden noise you make when you push air out of your throat (often when you are ill)

Sam has a bad cough, so he drinks warm water.

fever

n.

Meaning: a high body temperature when you are ill

Ada stayed in bed because she had a fever.

headache

n.

Meaning: a pain in your head

I got a headache after I played computer games for too long.

toothache

n.

Meaning: a pain in a tooth

Tom could not eat the apple because he had a toothache.

flu

n.

Meaning: an illness like a bad cold that makes you feel weak and hot

Several classmates caught the flu and missed school this week.

stomachache

n.

Meaning: a pain in your stomach

Lily had a stomachache after she ate too many sweets.

medicine

n.

Meaning: something you take to help you get better when you are ill

The doctor told me to take this medicine after breakfast.

Habits, character and chores

11 words

punctual

adj.

Meaning: arriving or doing something on time; not late

A punctual student always arrives at school before the bell.

regular

adj.

Meaning: happening often in the same way, or at the same times

She has regular piano lessons every Wednesday after school.

housework

n.

Meaning: jobs you do at home, such as cleaning and washing

Ben helps his mum with the housework, such as washing the dishes.

clumsy

adj.

Meaning: moving in a careless way and often dropping or breaking things

The clumsy boy dropped his water bottle on the floor.

embarrassed

adj.

Meaning: feeling shy or uncomfortable because of something you did or said

Amy felt embarrassed when she called her teacher the wrong name.

responsible

adj.

Meaning: able to be trusted to do the right thing, or in charge of something

A responsible class monitor checks that the windows are closed.

healthy

adj.

Meaning: strong and well; not ill

Eating fruit and vegetables every day helps us stay healthy.

encourage

v.

Meaning: to give someone hope or confidence so they keep trying

Our teacher likes to encourage us when we find the work difficult.

donation

n.

Meaning: money or things that you give to help people or a charity

The class made a donation of old books to the community centre.

patient

adj.

Meaning: able to wait calmly without getting angry

Please be patient and wait for your turn in the queue.

generous

adj.

Meaning: happy to give money, time, or help to others

The generous girl shared her snacks with her friends.

Disasters

6 words

typhoon

n.

Meaning: a very strong tropical storm with heavy rain and strong wind

A typhoon brought heavy rain and strong wind to Hong Kong yesterday.

flood

n.

Meaning: a large amount of water that covers land that is usually dry

After the long rain, there was a flood on the playground.

landslide

n.

Meaning: when a lot of earth and rocks slide down a hill or mountain

The heavy rain caused a landslide on the hillside road.

drought

n.

Meaning: a long time with little or no rain

During the drought, the farmers had little water for their plants.

earthquake

n.

Meaning: a sudden strong shaking of the ground

People walked outside carefully when they felt the earthquake shake the building.

severe

adj.

Meaning: very bad or serious

The city closed the schools because of the severe storm.
